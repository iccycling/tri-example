Bike

Cycling in London takes some planning

Cycling in London comes with its challenges. Small student rooms make storing, maintaining and cleaning a road bike difficult, the green roads are a long way away, traffic can be dangerous — and the academic year starts just when the days get short and the weather turns.

There is a good alternative: indoor cycling. ICTRI meets once a week for spinning together. You can also use the Wattbikes at the Ethos gym, which record precise power data — ideal for focused training sessions on your own.

And when the weather is kind, we ride outdoors: laps in Richmond Park, in a group, at a pace everyone can hold.

Spinning

Indoor sessions, outdoor results

Every Tuesday evening we meet at the Ethos gym for a spinning session. Music, structured intervals and a group to keep you honest — no matter the weather or the time of year.

No clip-in shoes or special kit needed: pick a bike, set the saddle and go. The session is a great base for the group rides.

Group rides

Laps in Richmond Park

On Wednesday evenings we ride laps of Richmond Park together. The park is closed to most traffic at the quiet hours and a lap is roughly 11 km — perfect for learning to ride in a group.

Groups are split by pace and nobody gets left behind. We ride predictably, call out hazards and look after each other.

Safety first. Helmets are compulsory. In autumn and winter bring front and rear lights. Check tyres, brakes and a spare tube before you come.
