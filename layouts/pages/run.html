Run

Lace up and go

Running is the most accessible sport in triathlon. You need a pair of shoes and a bit of time — no pool, no bike, no booking. That makes it the perfect place to start.

It is also the last discipline of a race, when tired legs from the bike meet everything you have left. The good news: you build that engine slowly, one easy run at a time.

Our runs are relaxed and social. We run together, talk, and finish with a coffee.

Running group

The ICTRI run club

Every Friday morning we run together in Hyde Park. We form groups by pace, so beginners and experienced runners can all join the same session and still meet at the end.

Most runs are 5–10 km at an easy, conversational pace. Now and then we add some strides or hills — always optional.
