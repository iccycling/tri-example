About the Club

Student-run, Union-backed, and open to every athlete at Imperial — from your first length to race day.

Who we are

A club for everyone who wants to tri

ICTRI is a student-led triathlon club at Imperial College London, affiliated with Imperial College Union. We swim, bike and run together — weekly sessions, open-water swims, BUCS races and plenty of socials in between.

You do not need to be fast, or to own a race bike. Some of us are chasing podiums, others are here to try a triathlon once. Everyone trains together.

The club is run entirely by students, so it is shaped by whoever joins. Want to organise a session or plan a trip? Just say so.
