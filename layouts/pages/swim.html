Swim

Where every triathlon starts

Swimming is the first discipline in triathlon — and the one most people find hardest, because good technique takes time to learn.

Races usually start in open water. Waves, murky visibility and a crowd of swimmers around you are a challenge of their own. The good news: the swim is the shortest part of a triathlon.

We run coached pool sessions for everyone — whether you want to learn front crawl to get through the swim of your first triathlon, or sharpen your technique as an experienced swimmer.

Open water swimming

When the weather warms up we swim outdoors at a safe, supervised venue. Open water is a different skill — sighting, cold and choppy water — and it is the best way to get ready for a race.

Not coached. Open-water swims are peer-led: we swim together, look after each other, and share tips. Dates and venue are announced in the members' WhatsApp group.
  • Wetsuit (strongly recommended) and a brightly coloured cap
  • Tow float — required for every swim
  • Comfortable swimming 400 m in the pool first
  • We always swim in a group, never alone
