Racing

Triathlon is everywhere

Triathlon is popular all over the world, and there are events of every distance almost everywhere you go. Racing is optional at ICTRI — but a start line is the best motivation to train.

You can race with the club or on your own. Ask around: somebody is usually planning an event and happy to share the journey and the car.

BUCS & local races

We race, together

The club takes part in a range of events during the year, from the BUCS championships to local races around London. Beginners and experienced athletes start side by side — and cheer each other on.

  • Travel togetherWe organise shared transport, so nobody arrives alone at 6 a.m.
  • Subsidised entriesThe club helps with entry fees for members who want to race.
  • News in WhatsAppConcrete events and deadlines are announced in the members' WhatsApp group.
  • Cheer and help outNot racing? Supporters and marshals are very welcome — and make the day.
Performance

Few sports leave so much room to optimise

Finding the right training volume and methods for your own body, testing your fitness, fuelling well on race day, choosing the right material, down to aerodynamic tuning in the wind tunnel: triathlon rewards curiosity.

No programme, but good company. The club cannot offer concrete performance services. But as a leading engineering university, Imperial has plenty of people who love exactly these technical topics — and are happy to exchange ideas.
  • TrainingVolume, intensity, zones — what works for your body.
  • Performance diagnosticsLactate, threshold or power tests show where you stand and set your training zones.
  • FuellingWhat and when to eat and drink before and during a race — a skill you can train like any other.
  • MaterialFrom second-hand bargains to the last watt on the bike.
  • AerodynamicsPosition, kit and the numbers behind them.
