Open water is different from the pool: no lane lines, no wall to rest on, and sometimes cold, murky water. A few habits make the first swim much easier.

## Kit

- A wetsuit (rent one before you buy)
- A brightly coloured cap
- A tow float, so others can see you

## Skills

Look up every ten strokes to sight, and breathe out steadily into the water. If you panic, roll onto your back and float.

{{< callout type="warning" title="Never swim alone" >}}
Always swim with the group and stay within reach of a safety kayak.
