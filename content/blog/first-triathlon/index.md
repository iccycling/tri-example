Your first triathlon is exciting, a bit chaotic and almost always better than you think. Here is what to expect.

## Before the race

Pick a **sprint** distance for your first one and register early. Practise the swim in open water at least once, and do a *brick session* (bike straight into a run) so your legs know what is coming.

## In transition

Lay out your kit in the order you will use it. Keep it simple: shoes, helmet, race number. Everything else slows you down.

> Nobody is looking at your transition. They are all worrying about their own.

## On the day

1. Arrive early and rack your bike.
2. Walk the swim exit and the run-in so you know where you are going.
3. Start in the back or on the side of the swim if you want space.

{{< callout type="tip" title="Not sure yet?" >}}
Come to a training session first. Most of us signed up for a race only after a few weeks with the club.
{{< /callout >}}

See you at the start line!
