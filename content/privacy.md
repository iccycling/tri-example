## In short

- This website **does not use cookies**, local storage, analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
- There are **no forms, logins or newsletters** on this website.
- Fonts and images are **hosted on this website**; nothing is loaded from third-party content networks.
- YouTube videos and Instagram posts are **only loaded after you click** and agree (see [embedded content](#embeds)).
- Because we do not use cookies or similar technologies that need consent, there is no cookie banner.

## 2. Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you visit the website, GitHub processes technical data, for example your IP address, the date and time of your visit, the page requested and information about your browser, in order to deliver and secure the website. We do not have access to this data and do not combine it with anything else.

**Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in providing a secure and reliable website (Art. 6(1)(f) UK GDPR / GDPR). GitHub explains how it handles data, including transfers to countries outside the UK/EEA, in its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## 3. Cookies and similar technologies

We do not set any cookies and we do not store information on your device. If you load embedded content from third parties (see below), those providers may set cookies themselves.

## 4. Embedded content (YouTube, Instagram) {#embeds}

Some pages can show videos from **YouTube** or posts from **Instagram**. They are blocked by default. You see a box with a short notice, and nothing is loaded from the provider until you click "Load".

When you click, your browser connects to the servers of the provider. This transmits data such as your IP address, the page you are on, browser information and the time of the request, and the provider may set cookies and recognise you if you are logged in. Data may be processed outside the UK/EEA, including in the USA.

- **YouTube** is operated for users in the UK and EEA by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. We use the privacy-enhanced domain youtube-nocookie.com. See Google's [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Instagram** is operated by Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, Merrion Road, Dublin 4, Ireland. See Instagram's [privacy policy](https://privacycenter.instagram.com/policy).

**Legal basis:** your consent by clicking (Art. 6(1)(a) UK GDPR / GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time by leaving or reloading the page and by deleting cookies in your browser settings.

## 5. Links, share buttons and social media

The share buttons are ordinary links. No data is sent to the networks when you only view a page; it happens only when you click a button, and the privacy policy of the respective service then applies. The same applies to links to other websites, such as Instagram, Strava, WhatsApp, LinkedIn or the Imperial College Union website. We have no influence on how these services process data.

## 6. Contact

If you contact us by email, Instagram or WhatsApp, we process the data you send (for example your name, contact details and message) to answer your request and to organise club activities. **Legal basis:** legitimate interests in answering enquiries and running the club, or steps taken at your request before joining (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)). We delete the data when it is no longer needed. Please do not send us sensitive data such as health information unless it is necessary.

Participation in the members' WhatsApp group is voluntary; WhatsApp is operated by Meta, see the [WhatsApp privacy policy](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy). Membership itself is handled by Imperial College Union, which has its own privacy information.

## 7. Photos and people

Names, roles and photos of committee members and authors are published with their agreement. Photos of members at events are published only if the people shown agree, or if they cannot be identified. If you appear in a photo and want it removed, write to us and we will remove it promptly.

## 8. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address above.

You can also complain to the supervisory authority. In the UK this is the [Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/). If you live in the EEA, you can contact the data protection authority in your country.

## 9. Changes

We may update this notice when the website or the law changes. The current version is always available on this page.
